# OVERSHOT

A free browser incremental about a water mill on a river that shifts with the seasons. Made for GitHub Game Off 2026, theme **SHIFT**.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari), or play it on the itch.io page. It runs in a desktop window, in the itch.io embed, and on a phone held upright or sideways. Nothing is installed and nothing leaves your browser.

One water wheel turns one line shaft. Every machine on the shaft (millstones, a saw frame, fulling stocks, a trip hammer, a paper beater) takes its drive through its own gearbox, and each one has a **band**: the green arc on its dial.

- Inside its band a machine works. Under it, it drags and makes little. Over it, it **races**: it wears, and if you leave it racing it breaks.
- Every machine you put **in gear** slows the whole shaft. A new machine can pull its neighbours under their bands.
- **Shift** a machine up (+) or down (−) to hold it in its band. Put it **out** of gear to rest it and free the shaft.
- The river **shifts** under you: autumn rains, a thin winter, a spring flood, a dry summer. A spate is forecast a day ahead.
- The **sluice** sets how much water reaches the wheel. A mill **pond** stores the flood for the drought.
- The carts bring work by season: grain in autumn, logs in winter, loom cloth in summer, bloom and rags all year. A machine short of work wears running empty.
- The manor wants its rent every quarter day, and it rises each year. Buy the **freehold** and the mill is yours.

If you miss two quarter days in a row, the steward ends the lease. You go back to Michaelmas (the start) of that year, with the mill as it stood then. Nothing from earlier years is lost.

### Controls

| | Mouse or touch | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| choose a machine | tap its bay or its row | 1 to 6, arrow left and right |
| shift up / down | + / − on its row | arrow up / down (or W / S) |
| in gear / out | IN GEAR / OUT | Space or Enter |
| sluice | − / + by the sluice | [ and ] |
| stop / speed | STOP, 1×, 2×, 4× | P, F |
| mill, shop, ledger | the tabs | Q, E, L |
| menu | MENU | Escape |
| sound | MENU, SOUND | M |

The game saves itself every few seconds and when you leave the page. **CONTINUE** on the title picks up where you were. The mill stands still while you are away: nothing turns, nothing wears.
